Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation is a government-registered Section 8 non-profit organization dedicated to grass-root social transformation, environmental welfare, and youth empowerment across India.

We work passionately across key pillars including education, healthcare, hunger relief, animal protection, and environmental conservation to drive real and measurable social impact.

Our Key Initiatives

Discover how we are driving impact through focused ongoing projects.

Social Welfare

Project Seva

Dedicated to eradicating hunger and homelessness by providing nutritious meals, clothing, and vital essentials to underprivileged families.

Education

Project Bachpanshala

Providing quality primary education, learning materials, and holistic growth opportunities to children in underserved communities.

Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Ensuring compassion for all living beings by daily rescue operations, healthcare support, and feeding stray animals in local communities.

Empowerment

Project Udaan

Focused on uplifting young women through digital literacy, skill development workshops, and leadership mentorship programs.

Sustainability

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental sustainability through massive tree plantation drives, waste management awareness, and clean-up campaigns.

Youth Skill Building

Project Vikas

Empowering young minds and students through structured internship opportunities in research, content writing, social work, and digital media.
